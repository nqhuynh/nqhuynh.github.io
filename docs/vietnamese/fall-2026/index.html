Learning goals

  • Recognize Vietnamese letters, vowel patterns, and written tone marks.
  • Read, pronounce, and write practiced syllables and short sentences.
  • Understand key details in a short spoken or written introduction.
  • Greet a classmate or teacher, exchange names, and say goodbye.

Materials & home practice

Bring a pencil and notebook. No textbook purchase is required. Each module includes explanations, examples, practice problems, homework, and reference links.

Practice assigned words in three short sessions of about five minutes between classes. Bring your notebook to the next class. Teachers give feedback; there are no letter grades.

HowToVietnamese provides pronunciation references and audio. MSU Basic Vietnamese provides additional practice and conversation units.
